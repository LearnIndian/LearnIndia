PRIVACY

Your Data, Your Control 🔐

LearnIndia follows a privacy-first sharing approach.

Mission 4 Online Sharing

  • Your profile gets a unique LearnIndia ID stored on the server.
  • You choose which fields are public.
  • Email and phone are private by default.
  • The public API returns only intentionally shared fields.
  • The owner key is never placed inside the QR URL.
  • Private Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat or WhatsApp information is never scraped or bypassed.
  • Use HTTPS when deploying publicly, especially for camera access.
